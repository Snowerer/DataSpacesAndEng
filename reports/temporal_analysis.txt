4. Temporal gaps: Yes, temporal gaps of 1 to 3 days exist between consecutive observations. This is normal due to the specific orbital revisit times of Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 satellites over the selected regions.

ENGINEERING CONCLUSION:
- Does the federated catalog provide continuous monitoring?
  No, satellite Earth Observation provides periodic (discrete) monitoring based on orbital passes, not continuous real-time video feeds.
- Are there periods without observations?
  Yes, depending on the orbit, there are gaps of 24-72 hours where no satellite passed over the Area of Interest (AOI).
- Would additional sensors improve temporal coverage?
  Yes. Incorporating additional commercial satellite constellations (like PlanetScope, which has daily revisits) or more SAR satellites would significantly reduce temporal gaps.
